# The Mock Mind — Learning Management Portal
## Client User Guide & Access Manual

Welcome to **The Mock Mind Learning Portal**. This guide provides simple, step-by-step instructions on how to access and use the platform for Administrators, Mentors, and Students.

---

- **Super Admin Login (Private Link)**: [http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in/portal-admin-x7k2/login](http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in/portal-admin-x7k2/login)

## 2. Super Admin Guide (System Administrator)

The Super Admin is responsible for setting up courses, creating student and mentor accounts, and overseeing the entire portal.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. **Log In to Admin Console**:
   - Go to the private link: `http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in/portal-admin-x7k2/login`
   - Enter your Admin username and password.

2. **Creating a New Student Account**:
   - Click the **Add Student** button at the top of the dashboard.
   - Enter the student's **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Address**, and select their **Course**.
   - Click **Create & Issue Credentials**.
   - A pop-up box will display a unique **Login ID** and **Password** (e.g., `student_sam` / `StudentPassword123!`).
   - Copy these login details and send them directly to the student (via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email).

3. **Creating a Mentor Account**:
   - Click the **Add Mentor** button.
   - Fill in the mentor's Name, Email, and Phone Number.
   - The system will auto-generate their login credentials to share with the mentor.

4. **Creating Courses**:
   - Go to the **Courses** tab and click **Add New Course**.
   - Enter the Course Title (e.g., *Full Stack Web Development*) and Course Code.

5. **Viewing System Overview (Source of Truth)**:
   - Use the **Students** tab to see all enrolled students across all courses, their current grades, assigned mentors, and uploaded resumes.

---

## 3. Mentor Guide (Instructors)

Mentors manage evaluation records and track progress for students enrolled in their assigned courses.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. **Log In as a Mentor**:
   - Visit the main website [http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in](http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in).
   - Click **Login** in the top menu and choose **Mentor Login**.
   - Enter the Login ID and Password provided by your Administrator.

2. **Viewing Assigned Students**:
   - Once logged in, your dashboard displays only the students enrolled in your course(s).
   - You can see each student's name, email, phone number, and current overall grade.

3. **Updating Student Scores & Feedback**:
   - Click **Update Scores & Grade** on any student's card.
   - Enter or adjust the scores:
     - **Assignment Score** (e.g., `90/100`)
     - **Test / Quiz Score** (e.g., `85/100`)
     - **Project Score** (e.g., `95/100`)
     - **Overall Grade** (e.g., `A+` or `Excellent`)
     - **Mentor Remarks** (Add personalized feedback and notes)
   - Click **Save & Append to Log**.
   - *Note: All updates automatically save to the student's progress history timeline so progress can be tracked over time.*

4. **Viewing Student Resumes**:
   - If a student has uploaded a resume, a **View PDF** button will appear on their card allowing you to open and review their document.

---

## 4. Student Guide (Learners)

Students can log in to view their course progress, evaluation scores, mentor remarks, and upload their resume.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. **Log In as a Student**:
   - Visit the main website [http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in](http://themochdash.cyberpunk.co.in).
   - Click **Login** in the top menu and choose **Student Login**.
   - Enter the **Login ID** and **Password** issued to you by your Administrator.

2. **Viewing Your Portfolio & Course Status**:
   - Your dashboard shows your registered contact details, enrolled course name, and assigned mentor.

3. **Checking Your Grades & Feedback**:
   - The **Course Performance Scores** card displays your live Assignment, Test/Quiz, Project, and Overall Grade entered by your mentor.
   - Read your mentor's personalized feedback under **Mentor Remarks**.
   - Scroll down to the **Performance History Log** to see how your grades have improved over time.

4. **Uploading / Updating Your Resume**:
   - Locate the **Resume & Portfolio File** box on your dashboard.
   - Click **Upload Resume** (or **Replace Resume** if updating an existing one).
   - Select your resume file from your computer or phone (accepted formats: PDF, DOC, or DOCX up to 10MB).
   - Once uploaded, click **View Resume** at any time to inspect your uploaded document.
